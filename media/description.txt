Trail Predictor

Know how long your trail will take — while you're still on it.

Trail Predictor shows two values on your watch during an activity:

- ETE — estimated time en route: how much time is left until you finish
- ETT — estimated total time: your finish time (elapsed time + ETE)

The prediction adapts to terrain. It uses your average pace over distance, weighted for climbing and descending, so it accounts for how trails really feel — flat kilometers run differently than steep ones.

How it gets your remaining route:

1. Navigating a route (recommended): select a route in navigation and start moving — Trail Predictor reads the remaining distance, ascent, and descent automatically and updates as you go.
2. No route? Set your planned distance, ascent, and descent in the app settings in the Suunto app beforehand, and the app compares your plan against your progress.

To use this app you need:

- For best results: a planned route to navigate, or known plan values entered in the app settings
- About a minute of activity before predictions appear — the app waits until it has enough real movement data to give you a reliable estimate

Predictions assume you keep moving at roughly the pace you've built up so far. Big pace changes (a steep climb after a fast start) will make the estimate adjust as your average catches up.
